Frequently Asked Questions

How do I subscribe or sign in to Forsa TV?

Open the app, tap "Sign In" or "Get Started" on the home screen, and follow the on-screen steps to create or access your account.

The app is not playing videos, what should I do?

Check your internet connection, make sure the app is updated to the latest version from the App Store, and restart the app. If the issue continues, contact us via email or WhatsApp above.

How do I report a bug or technical issue?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or email with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version, and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

How can I request a feature or give feedback?

We'd love to hear from you. Reach out via email or WhatsApp with your suggestions.

How do I delete my account or data?

Contact us via email at rakan.developer@gmail.com with your account details and we will process your request promptly.
